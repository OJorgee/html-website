These games mimic real-life experiences as accurately as possible, rewarding methodical planning and management over fast reflexes.

Their Focus: Replicating real-world systems, professions, or physics.

Some exaples of the genres:
  • Life / Farming Simulation: Managing the day-to-day routines, social lives, or agricultural businesses of virtual people.
  • Construction and Management (Tycoon Games): You act as an omnipotent architect or manager, balancing finances and infrastructure to run a city, an amusement park, or a zoo.
  • Vehicle Simulation: Replicating the highly complex, mathematically accurate controls of real-world machines like commercial airplanes, trains, or trucks.
