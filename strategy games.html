You rarely control a single character. Instead, you act as a general, a god, or a world leader commanding entire armies, nations, or civilizations from a top-down view.

Their Focus: High-level tactical planning and resource management.

Some exaples of the genres:
  • Real-Time Strategy (RTS): Both you and your opponent act simultaneously in real-time. You must harvest resources, build bases, and command troops at a frantic pace.
  • Turn-Based Strategy (TBS): Players take turns making their moves, allowing for deep, calculated thinking without the stress of a ticking clock.
  • Tower Defense: A sub-genre where you place stationary defensive structures along a set path to stop waves of automated enemies from reaching your base.
