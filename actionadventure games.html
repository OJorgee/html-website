Because pure adventure games can feel too slow for some, and pure action games can feel too mindless, this hybrid is arguably the most popular macro-genre in modern gaming. It blends physical combat and nimble movement with deep storylines and exploration.

Their Focus: The middle ground.

Some exaples of the genres:
  • Survival Horror: Action-adventure games specifically designed to make you feel vulnerable, featuring scarce resources, terrifying atmospheres, and terrifying creatures.
  • Metroidvania: Games featuring a massive, interconnected map where certain pathways are blocked until you find specific tools or abilities later in your journey.
